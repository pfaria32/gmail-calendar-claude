Gmail and Calendar - claude

This is a private, single-user tool. It reads and organises the mail and calendar of its owner's own Google account: it applies labels, archives messages according to the owner's rules, and reads calendar entries to keep a personal task list.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users. Only the owner's own Google account is connected to it.
