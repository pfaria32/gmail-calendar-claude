Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026.

"Gmail and Calendar - claude" is a private tool used by one person, its owner, on the owner's own Google account. It has no other users.

Data the tool accesses

With the owner's consent the tool accesses Gmail messages and labels (read, label, archive, mark as read) and Google Calendar entries (read only) of the owner's account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to sort the owner's mail and to keep the owner's personal task list. It is processed on a server the owner controls.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties, except service providers the owner uses to process their own data on their own behalf. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are kept in the owner's password manager. The owner can revoke access at any time in the Google Account settings under "Third-party apps and services", and can delete the stored data on their own server.
